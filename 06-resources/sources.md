## Caveats
- Exam pattern and eligibility criteria are **not on a fixed public schedule** — Infosys hiring depends on organizational needs batch to batch. Treat everything in `02-syllabus-and-pattern/` as the most recently reported pattern, and reconfirm against your own official hiring communication.
- Salary figures are self-reported/aggregated across sources and will vary by drive, location, and level.
